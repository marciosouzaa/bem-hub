# Commercial Validation

## First 30 Conversations

Interview 15 to 30 SMBs before building advanced integrations.

Questions:
- Which repetitive tasks consume the most time every week?
- Which documents does the team consult repeatedly?
- Who already uses ChatGPT or another AI tool at work?
- Are there concerns about employees pasting sensitive data into AI tools?
- Which task would justify R$ 99, R$ 199, or R$ 399 per month?
- Which department has the clearest pain: support, sales, finance, HR, or operations?

## Pilot Offers

Avoid selling a generic AI platform. Package by routine:

- BEM HUB for Support: answers, knowledge base, history.
- BEM HUB for Sales: proposals, follow-ups, lead analysis.
- BEM HUB for Accounting: document summaries, checklists, internal Q&A.
- BEM HUB for Real Estate: property descriptions, client replies, document analysis.

## 90-Day Targets

- 20 company conversations.
- 5 hands-on pilots.
- 3 paying customers.
- 1 segment with clear traction.
- 10 templates with repeated usage.

## Metrics

- Weekly active users.
- Messages per organization.
- Documents uploaded per organization.
- Template runs.
- Retention by week.
- Conversion from pilot to paid.
