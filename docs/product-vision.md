# BEM HUB Product Vision

BEM HUB is an operational AI workspace for Brazilian SMBs.

The product centralizes official assistants, company documents, manual automations, usage history, permissions, and future integrations in one workspace. The first commercial promise is practical:

> Put AI to work in your company processes with assistants, documents, integrations, and automations in one place.

## Target Customer

- Brazilian SMBs with 5 to 200 employees.
- Initial segments: accounting firms, real estate agencies, clinics, marketing agencies, e-commerces, service providers, and recurring support operations.
- Main buyer: owner, manager, or operations coordinator who wants productivity without hiring a technical team.

## MVP Positioning

Start as a corporate AI chat with official assistants and company documents. Agents, advanced automations, marketplace, WhatsApp, CRM, ERP, and white-label are later expansion paths.

## Success Criteria

- A company can create a workspace, invite members, create assistants, upload documents, and ask questions with history.
- Users understand value in less than 10 minutes.
- At least 3 pilot customers pay for the first version or commit to a paid pilot.
- The product preserves tenant isolation through Supabase RLS from the first schema.
